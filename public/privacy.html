Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ApplyStack is a free, open-source app for tracking job applications. Each copy of it is run by one person (the "admin") who shares it with friends. This page explains what this site stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, when you sign in with Google. We use them only to create your account and show you who you are signed in as. We don't ask Google for anything else.
  • What you put in the app: jobs, job descriptions, notes, statuses, resumes, profile details and AI chat history.
  • An AI provider API key, if you add one on the Profile tab, so the app can make AI requests for you. It is stored encrypted (see below), and is never sent back to your browser; you only ever see its last four characters.
  • A login cookie that keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How it is used and shared

  • Your data is used only to run the app for you. Other users can't see it.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text it needs (for example a job description and your resume) is sent to the AI provider you chose, using your own key. That provider's privacy policy applies to it.
  • The app is hosted on Vercel and its database is on Turso, which store the data on our behalf.
  • The admin runs the database that holds your jobs, resumes and profile, so only use this site if you trust the person who shared it with you.

How your API key is protected

  • Your AI key, your Claude connection token, and your Google Drive connection if you turn it on, are encrypted before they are saved. The database only holds scrambled text; the encryption key is kept separately, in the server's settings, not in the database. So nobody browsing the database, or holding a copy of it, can read your key, the admin included.
  • The key is decrypted only inside the server, at the moment a request is sent to your AI provider, and is never written to logs or shown in full.
  • As with any service you give an API key to, the server itself has to be trusted. Use a key you can delete from the provider's website at any time, and with paid providers, set a spending limit. You can remove your key from ApplyStack by deleting it from the provider's website, or by asking the admin to delete your account.

Google user data

ApplyStack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. If you turn on the optional Google Drive backup, the app can only see files it created itself, in an "ApplyStack Resumes" folder in your Drive (named "Job Tracker Resumes" if you connected Drive before the rename). You can disconnect it at any time from your Google account permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you or the admin delete it. You can delete jobs and resumes yourself in the app. To have your whole account deleted, ask the admin (the person who shared this site with you), and they will remove it from the Users tab.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too. The app's source code is public on GitHub, so you can check exactly what it does with your data.
